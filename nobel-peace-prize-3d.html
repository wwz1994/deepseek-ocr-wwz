Nobel Peace Prize

The Nobel Peace Prize is one of the five Nobel Prizes established by the will of Swedish industrialist, inventor, and armaments manufacturer Alfred Nobel.

First Awarded: 1901

Purpose: To honor those who have "done the most or the best work for fraternity between nations, for the abolition or reduction of standing armies and for the holding and promotion of peace congresses."

Prize: Gold medal, diploma, and monetary award

Location: Awarded in Oslo, Norway (other Nobel Prizes in Stockholm, Sweden)

Notable Laureates

  • Martin Luther King Jr. (1964)
  • Mother Teresa (1979)
  • Nelson Mandela (1993)
  • Malala Yousafzai (2014)
  • International Red Cross (3 times)
  • Wangari Maathai (2004)
  • Barack Obama (2009)
